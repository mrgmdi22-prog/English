English text: Edgar Allan Poe, «The Purloined Letter» (1844), from The Works of Edgar Allan Poe, Raven Edition, Volume 2, public domain (Project Gutenberg edition). Arabic translation: عباس محمود العقاد، «ألوان من القصة القصيرة في الأدب الأمريكي» (1963). Al-Aqqad translates freely: he skips a few short exchanges (the cellars, the French and Latin word-play) and ends before the two lines of French verse Dupin leaves inside the letter, so those English paragraphs share their Arabic with a neighbour.
